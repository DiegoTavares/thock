# Make Thock yours

Thock arrives with one look and one set of keys — but none of it is fixed.
Five minutes here and it will feel like your desk, not someone else's.

_(On Linux, read `ctrl` wherever you see `⌘`.)_

## Your look

A theme picker should have just opened next to this page — move through it
with `↑` `↓` and watch the whole app change live. Dark is only the default:
there are light themes, warm ones, high-contrast ones. Press `enter` on the
one that feels right; your choice is saved.

Want it back later? `⌘ K ⌘ T` opens the theme picker any time.

## Your text

Too small or too large? `⌘ +` and `⌘ -` resize the text you're reading
right now. To make a size the default, ask your agent — *"make my note text
bigger"* — or change `buffer_font_size` in the settings (`⌘ ,`).

## Your language

Your notes and your agent can work in your language: open the command bar
(`⌘ ⇧ P`) and run **thock: set language**. Your agent translates the
templates, docs, and list headings with you — though Thock's own buttons
and menus stay in English for now.

## The keys worth knowing

- **⌘ ⇧ P — do anything.** Opens the command bar. Type what you want in
  plain words — `open today`, `run skill`, `toggle backlog` — and press
  `enter`. Everything Thock can do lives in there, so if you forget every
  other shortcut, this one is enough.
- **⌘ 2** — your days and rituals (the left rail). Move with `↑` `↓`,
  press `enter` to open a note or run a ritual.
- **⌘ ⌥ P** — your day, drawn (the planner beside a daily note).
- **⌘ ⌥ U** — your lists (the Backlog along the bottom).
- **⌘ ⌥ T** — your agent.
- **esc** — back to your note. From any panel, `esc` returns you to
  writing.

And while writing:

- **⌘ W** — close the current note.
- **⌘ ⌥ M** — show the markup. Notes normally tuck away symbols like `#`
  and `[ ]`; this shows the raw text when you're curious.

## Change the keys too

Every shortcut can be rebound — they live in a small settings file, like
everything else in Thock.

- **Ask your agent.** Open it (`⌘ ⌥ T`), start a conversation, and say
  something like *"change my backlog shortcut to cmd-alt-b"*. The keyboard
  settings file lives at `~/.config/thock/keymap.json`.
- **Or do it yourself.** `⌘ ⇧ P` → `zed: open keymap` shows every binding
  and lets you change them in place.
