An introduction

Your notes. Your agent. Your machine.

Thock is a home for the things on your mind — days, plans, lists, reflections — kept as plain files on your own computer, with an AI assistant that helps you keep the rhythm.

What Thock believes

Your files, forever

Everything you write is an ordinary file in an ordinary folder. There is no account holding your notes hostage, no special format. If Thock disappeared tomorrow, every word would still open on any computer.

It adds — it never rewrites

When your assistant reviews your day or your week, it writes its part below yours, as its own section. Your words are never reworded, reordered, or deleted. What you wrote stays exactly as you wrote it.

You confirm anything that matters

The assistant proposes; you decide. It will suggest where a captured note should be filed or what to carry into tomorrow — and then it waits for your yes. Nothing important happens without you.

Nothing is ever lost

Thock quietly keeps every version of every note as you work. If anything ever goes wrong — a bad edit, an accident — your words can come back. You never have to think about it.

Four words to know

Vault
The folder holding all your notes. This one lives at ~/Thock.
Agent
The AI assistant you already use, running beside your notes. You bring your own; Thock just gives it a seat and the house rules.
Ritual
A small routine your agent runs when you ask — wrapping up the day, reviewing the week, sorting what you've captured. Each one is a readable file you can open and change.
Routine
A bundle of rituals and folders for one part of life — your days and weeks, your inbox, your finances. Add the ones you want; skip the rest.
