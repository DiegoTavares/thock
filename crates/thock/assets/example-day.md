## How this page works

- `- [ ]` makes a checkbox. Put a time in front and it appears on the planner to the right.
- `## ` starts a section, `___` draws a line, `**bold**` and `_italic_` do what they say, and `> ` quotes.
- Double brackets link to another note: [[welcome]].
- Your agent adds its part *below* yours and never rewrites what you wrote. Try **Wrap Today** in the left rail tonight.
