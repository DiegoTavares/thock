# Triage policy

The judgment the **Triage Inbox** ritual and the Inbox view's suggestions follow. These are the
shipped defaults — run **Set Up Inbox** to replace them with your own answers, or just edit this
file; the ritual reads it fresh on every run. Once **Refresh Inbox Policy** runs, it keeps the
Proposals and Criteria from your notes and decisions.

## Proposals

| If it looks like… | Propose |
| --- | --- |
| A link or article to read | Backlog · Someday, as a task carrying the link |
| A raw idea or thought | Backlog · Someday |
| Something with a `due:` date within a week, or clearly urgent wording | Backlog · Soon |
| A note about a project or topic that has its own folder or Routine in this vault | Append to that project's note, or file the whole note there |
| Something that reads like today's business (an errand, a call, a follow-up) | Today's note, under the Day planner section |
| Obvious noise (a test capture, an empty item, a duplicate of something already filed) | Discard |

## Criteria

What each of the suggestion's five answers means, one line each.

- **today**: Something to do, call, or read today, or a line for today's page.
- **soon**: A task for the coming days: `item.due_in` is within a week, or the wording is urgent.
- **someday**: A link, article, idea or task with no date pressure.
- **archive**: Nothing for this person to act on: a notification, receipt, newsletter, test
  capture or duplicate, or a ticket that belongs to another team under the policy.
- **unsure**: None of the above fits, or the item is a note that belongs in a folder of its own.

## Destinations

Tasks go to `backlog.md` under its **Soon** or **Someday** heading. Lines for today go into
today's daily note. Notes kept whole are moved into the folder that fits them best — offer
only folders that actually exist in this vault. There is no reading-list note configured;
links ride along on their Backlog task until Set Up Inbox says otherwise.

## When unsure

Propose **Backlog · Someday** and say why you weren't sure. Never guess a destination into a
folder that doesn't exist, and never invent a new folder without asking.
