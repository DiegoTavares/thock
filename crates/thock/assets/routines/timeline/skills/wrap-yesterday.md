# Wrap Yesterday

Close out **yesterday's** daily note — the retrospective you didn't get to before bed.

This is the [Wrap Today](./wrap-today.md) ritual run against yesterday instead of today. Follow every step in that skill, with these substitutions:

- **The target day is the last day that was written:** the most recent day before today whose daily note has anything beyond the template, looking back at most three days (on a Monday that is usually Friday). With no such note, it is yesterday (today minus one day): still create its note, and note that the day went uncaptured. The note follows the same config-resolved naming as Wrap Today, anything step 3 pulls in is that day's, and the review appends to that day's note.
- **The context scan** (step 4) reads the 2–3 daily notes *before* yesterday, plus today's note if it already exists, to catch threads that lingered into today.
- **The sheet** (step 5) sorts yesterday's loose ends: yesterday's unfinished tasks and the reminders due yesterday. Leave out any task the user has visibly picked up again in today's note — it isn't lingering, it's in progress. The reminder choices keep their meaning from today: **Tomorrow 09:00** is the day after today.

Everything else is identical to Wrap Today: reading tasks, the profile-gated pull-in step (it looks only where `profile.md` allows, and skips itself in silence when nothing is allowed), the one `sort` sheet and how its answers are written (reminders on their own line, backlog moves deduplicated), the `# Daily Closure` section format, the Reflect step (daily mode, over yesterday's note), and the output.
